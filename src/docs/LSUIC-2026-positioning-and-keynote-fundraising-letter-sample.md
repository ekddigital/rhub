Dear **Dr. Jane M. Doe**,

On behalf of the Liberian Student Union in China (LSUIC), we write with respect.

We respectfully invite you to deliver the **keynote address** at a virtual fundraising session benefiting the **LSUIC Jinan 2026 Conference Fundraising Campaign** and the broader **Liberian student community in China**. The session is scheduled for **May 29, 2026** at **21:00 (China time)**, via **Zoom**; we propose an approximate duration of **15–20 minutes**, subject to your availability.

The thematic focus for this milestone is *"Honoring Our Past, Engaging Our Present, and Inspiring Our Future"*. We welcome remarks that reinforce this vision, engage students and supporting partners constructively, and help inspire concrete support so that peers who rely on pooled resources can take part.

**Proposed thematic emphasis (for your consideration):**  
*Connect the “Jinan 2026: Legacy and Influence” framing with student leadership and stewardship in the diaspora—grounded in Peace, Togetherness, Love, Support, Willingness, Passion, and Service—so the audience sees a clear through-line to reducing barriers to conference participation.*

We are asking for **both** your voice on this programme and your **meaningful backing of our community**. Alongside delivering the keynote, we earnestly invite a **financial or comparable contribution** aligned with what you can offer; the overview and payment channels below show how donations directly ease fees and sustain the conference for Liberian students in China.

Each year, LSUIC convenes this conference to bring together Liberian students across China for leadership development, mentorship, professional networking, and national service planning. It offers a structured forum where students from many cities can meet, learn, and strengthen practical support networks.

As a student-led organization, we operate under significant resource constraints. Many members are not fully funded; some rely on partial scholarships, while others face considerable financial pressures. With limited paid employment during study, conference-related costs can prevent participation.

Our present objective is to **secure support that will help reduce conference fees and enable more Liberian students to attend.**
