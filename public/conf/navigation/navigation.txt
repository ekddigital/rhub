5. Critical Bus K904 Rules for All Attendees
 
This bus is mandatory for all public transport routes to the hotel; read carefully:
 
1. Final metro stop before bus transfer: 济泺路地铁站 Jile Road Metro Station (Line 2, Exit B).
2. Transfer flow: Exit Jile Road Metro Station Exit B, walk 381m / 6 minutes → 长途汽车站(长途总站北区) → Bus K904 toward 齐河驾考中心 → ride 9 stops → 国科国际 stop → short walk to hotel.
3. Operating Window: K904 runs 6:00 AM – 19:20.
If you arrive at Jile Road Metro Station later than around 19:10, you will miss the final K904 bus and must use DiDi/Taxi.
4. Frequency: Departs every 20 minutes (plan arrival at bus stop ahead of time to avoid long waits)
5. Fare: Fixed ¥6 cash / mobile pay accepted
6. Destination Direction Confirmation: Always board K904 marked Toward 齐河驾考中心
7. Drop-off Stop: Alight at 国科国际 (Guoke Guoji) – do not miss this stop
8. Last Leg Walk: After exiting 国科国际 stop, short walk straight to hotel entrance
 
6. Walking Directions: From the Bus Stop to Hotel
 
After stepping off K904 at 国科国际 (Guoke Guoji) Bus Stop:
 
1. Head north on the main roadside pedestrian walkway
2. Pass the commercial plaza on your left
3. Continue straight for 8 minutes; the large Arcadia Spa Golf International Hotel main gate will be visible on your right
4. Enter through the main lobby entrance for conference check-in
