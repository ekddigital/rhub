20th Annual Conference
Jinan, Shandong Province, People’s Republic of China

1. Executive Summary
The Cooking Committee of the Liberian Student Union in China (LSUIC) was entrusted with the responsibility of planning, procuring, preparing, and serving meals for delegates and guests attending the 20th Annual Conference held in Jinan, Shandong Province, from 24 to 27 July 2026.
A total amount of RMB 18,113.03 was disbursed to the Committee to cover the purchase of food items, cooking ingredients, kitchen utensils, transportation, and other operational expenses necessary for the successful execution of the conference catering activities.
According to the Committee’s financial records, RMB 17,538.08 was expended, leaving an unspent balance of RMB 574.95.
The Committee maintained expenditure records for all purchases and transfers made during the conference period.

5. Financial Position
The Cooking Committee successfully executed its assigned responsibilities during the 20th Annual Conference while operating within the approved budget.
The Committee received a total allocation of RMB 18,113.03 and utilized RMB 17,538.08 in support of conference feeding operations, procurement of food supplies, cooking ingredients, kitchen utensils, cleaning materials, transportation, and reimbursements made to members who advanced funds or purchased approved items on behalf of the Committee.
