1. Key Travel Hub Overview
 
All conference attendees will arrive at one of 3 Jinan train stations; travel distance & time varies drastically:
 
1. Jinan West Railway Station (济南西站) – TOP RECOMMENDED ARRIVAL STATION
Driving time: 23–32 mins | Toll fee: ¥10–20 | Closest to the hotel
2. Jinan Railway Station (济南站, City Center)
Driving time: ~36 mins | Toll fee: ¥10
3. Jinan East Railway Station (济南东站) – Farthest station
Driving time: 38–50 mins | Toll fee: ¥10–22
 
All public transit routes end with taking Bus K904 to reach the hotel.
 
2. Route 1: Jinan West Railway Station (Recommended)
 
Public Transit (Subway Line 4 → Line 2 → K904 Bus)
 
Total estimated time: ~70–80 mins | Total transit fare: ¥10 (Subway ¥4 + Bus K904 ¥6)
Step 1: Walk 4 minutes (900ft) from station exit to Jinanxi Railway Station West Square Subway Station
Step 2: Board Metro Line 4, direction Pengjiazhuang
- Operating Hours: 6:05 AM – 10:35 PM | Trains every 12 mins
- Ride 3 stops (8 mins), exit at Lashan Station
Step 3: 1-minute indoor transfer to Metro Line 2
Step 4: Board Metro Line 2, direction Pengjiazhuang
- Operating Hours: 6:06 AM – 10:26 PM | Trains every 10 mins | Free subway transfer
- Ride 5 stops (11 mins), exit at Jinan Railway Station North (Exit B)
Step 5: Walk 5 minutes (0.2 miles) to Jinan Long-distance Transport Center Bus Stop (Dikou Lu)
Step 6: Board Bus K904, direction Qihe Jiakao Zhongxin
- Operating Hours: 6:00 AM – 7:20 PM | Buses every 20 mins | Fare ¥6
- Ride 9 stops (43 mins), exit at Guoke Guoji Bus Stop
Step 7: 8-minute walk (0.3 miles) to Arcadia Spa Golf International Hotel

5. Critical Bus K904 Rules for All Attendees
 
This bus is mandatory for all public transit routes to the hotel; read carefully:
 
1. Operating Window: Only runs 6:00 AM – 7:20 PM.
If you arrive after 7:20 PM, NO public bus service available – you must take a taxi/car to the hotel.
2. Frequency: Departs every 20 minutes (plan arrival at bus stop ahead of time to avoid long waits)
3. Fare: Fixed ¥6 cash / mobile pay accepted
4. Destination Direction Confirmation: Always board K904 marked Toward Qihe Jiakao Zhongxin
5. Drop-off Stop: Alight at Guoke Guoji (stop after YuanLinChang) – do not miss this stop
6. Last Leg Walk: After exiting Guoke Guoji stop, 8-minute flat walk straight to hotel entrance (0.3 miles)
 
6. Hotel Final Walking Directions (From Guoke Guoji Bus Stop)
 
After stepping off K904 at Guoke Guoji Bus Stop:
 
1. Head north on the main roadside pedestrian walkway
2. Pass the commercial plaza on your left
3. Continue straight for 8 minutes; the large Arcadia Spa Golf International Hotel main gate will be visible on your right
4. Enter through the main lobby entrance for conference check-in
